About the group

One family. Many companies. One standard of quality.

South Bangla Group owns and runs a growing set of companies. Some grow food, some make clothes, some spin jute, some build homes, and one welcomes guests to a green resort. Each company has its own team and its own work — and all of them carry the same promise.

That promise is in the group’s own words: sustainable growth, innovation and quality — connecting modern business with nature, and being a good partner to every client, supplier and employee.

  • ✦
    Quality firstCareful checks at every step, from raw material to finished product.
  • ✦
    Promises keptOn-time delivery and honest dealings with every customer.
  • ✦
    Respect for natureGreen factories, farm waste reused, eco-friendly methods.
  • ✦
